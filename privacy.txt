PRIVACY NOTICE

Last updated May 10, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains what information troots collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and what choices you have.

If you do not agree with this notice, please do not use troots.
If you have questions, contact: contact.aneeska@gmail.com.

SUMMARY

- We collect account and family-tree information needed to run troots.
- We use trusted service providers for sign-in, cloud sync, and subscriptions.
- We do not include third-party ad trackers in the app source.
- You can sign out or delete your account from inside the app.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

A. Account information
- User ID
- Email address
- Display name
- Profile photo URL (if available from your sign-in provider)
- Sign-in provider details

B. Family-tree content you add
- Tree titles and descriptions
- Family member names and relationships
- Optional details such as birth/death labels, location, phone number, notes
- Profile photos you choose to upload
- Collaboration and approval history needed for shared editing

C. Subscription information
- Current plan/tier status
- Purchase entitlement state from payment/subscription processors

D. Local app data on your device
- Cached tree data and settings
- Local photo files used by the app
- Session/account snapshot used to keep your experience stable

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use data to:
- Sign you in and keep your account working
- Save and sync your trees across devices/collaborators
- Upload and manage profile photos
- Enforce plan limits and process subscription features
- Support account deletion and reliability/safety of the service

3. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

We share information only with service providers needed to operate troots, such as:
- Authentication and cloud database providers
- File/media storage and processing providers
- Subscription and payment-platform processors
- Identity providers used for sign-in

We do not sell your personal information.

4. PERMISSIONS AND DEVICE ACCESS

troots may request access to:
- Photos: when you choose a profile photo
- Contacts (optional): when you choose to import a contact phone number/name
- Internet: for sign-in, sync, and subscription features

You can control permissions in your device settings.

5. HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA

We keep data as long as needed to provide troots, unless you delete it or request deletion, and subject to legal/backup limits.

Data may exist:
- On your device (local database/files)
- In cloud systems used to run troots

6. ACCOUNT DELETION AND DATA DELETION

troots includes an in-app account deletion flow.
When completed, we attempt to:
- Remove your account index records
- Remove or detach your shared/owned tree access and related cloud data
- Delete related remote photos associated with your account activity
- Delete local app data tied to that account

Some backups or provider-side retention windows may delay full purge.

7. SECURITY

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your data and restrict access.
No online system can promise 100% security, but we work to reduce risk and limit access to authorized use.

8. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

You can:
- Sign out at any time
- Delete your account in-app
- Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion support

Depending on your location, you may also have additional legal privacy rights.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

troots is a family-tree app and may contain information you choose to enter about family members.
Only add information you are authorized to share.

10. DO-NOT-TRACK

troots does not currently respond to browser Do-Not-Track signals.

11. CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

We may update this notice from time to time.
When we do, we update the "Last updated" date.
